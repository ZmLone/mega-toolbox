Case Converter

Easily transform your text to any standard capitalization format.

How to Use the Case Converter Tool

Accidentally left your caps lock on? Or need to format an essay title perfectly? Our free online case converter instantly transforms your text without requiring manual retyping. Simply paste your document into the text box and select your desired format.

Available Formats

  • UPPERCASE: Converts every single letter to a capital letter. Useful for emphasizing warnings or formatting specific database entries.
  • lowercase: Transforms all characters to small letters. Ideal for cleaning up inconsistently typed data or preparing text for URL slugs.
  • Title Case: Capitalizes the first letter of every major word. Perfect for blog post titles, book covers, and academic headings.
  • Sentence case: Capitalizes only the first letter of the first word in a sentence, leaving the rest lowercase (except proper nouns). This is standard for paragraphs and standard reading text.
