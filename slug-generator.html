Text to Slug Converter

Transform article titles into clean, valid URL slugs for your website.

What is a URL Slug?

A URL slug is the exact part of a web address that identifies a specific page on a website in an easy-to-read format. For example, in the URL megatools.com/text-utilities/text-to-slug, the string text-to-slug is the slug.

Why Use a Slug Generator?

Browsers and web servers cannot read spaces or special characters like commas, apostrophes, or emojis in a URL string without "encoding" them (turning a space into %20). If you use a raw title as a URL, it becomes incredibly messy. Our tool automatically formats your text to standard web conventions:

  • Converts all letters to lowercase.
  • Replaces all spaces with hyphens (-).
  • Strips out punctuation marks, ampersands, and special symbols.
  • Removes leading or trailing hyphens.
