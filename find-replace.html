Bulk String Editing Made Easy

Sometimes you need to update a company name across a 50-page legal document, or change formatting tags in HTML markup. Doing this by hand invites human error. Our online Find and Replace tool lets you target exact character strings and swap them globally in milliseconds.

How Match Case Works

By default, searching for "apple" will also replace "Apple" or "APPLE". If you only want to replace the exact capitalization you type into the "Find" field, ensure you check the Match Case box before hitting replace.
